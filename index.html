About Viki

Viki had a gift for making any kitchen feel like home. These recipes — written in her own hand on index cards and notepads — represent a lifetime of cooking for the people she loved.

From weeknight casseroles to holiday cookies, each dish carries a memory. This collection was made so that her recipes, and the warmth behind them, will never be forgotten.
