Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Viki's Kitchen is a personal, non-commercial memorial website dedicated to preserving the recipes of Viki Tozier. We take your privacy seriously and want to be straightforward about how this site works.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information. This site has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, no comment sections, and no analytics tracking of our own.

Third-Party Services

This site uses a small number of third-party services that may collect limited technical information as part of their normal operation:

  • GitHub Pages — This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server log data (such as IP addresses and browser type) as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts — We use Google Fonts to load typefaces. When your browser loads this page, it makes a request to Google's servers, which may log your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Cookies

We do not set any cookies. GitHub Pages or Google Fonts may set cookies as part of their own services — please refer to their respective privacy policies linked above.

Email Contact

If you choose to email us at recipes@vikiskitchen.com, the content of your message and your email address will be used only to respond to you. We will not share your information with anyone.

Children's Privacy

This site is a family recipe archive intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at recipes@vikiskitchen.com.
